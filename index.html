Personal reminders by text

Reminders that show up where you’ll see them.

TextMe Reminders is a personal reminder service that sends user-created reminders by SMS. Reply to complete a reminder or snooze it for later.

Example reminder
TextMe Reminders: Reminder — Take the garbage out. Reply DONE when completed or SNOOZE 1H to be reminded again in one hour. Reply STOP to opt out.
Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

How it works

1

Create a reminder

Choose what you want to remember, when you want the reminder, and whether it repeats.

2

Receive a text

At the scheduled time, TextMe Reminders sends the reminder to your opted-in mobile number.

3

Reply to act

Reply with supported commands such as DONE or SNOOZE 1H. Reply STOP at any time to opt out.

SMS opt-in

To subscribe to SMS reminders, text START to YOUR_TWILIO_NUMBER.

By texting START, you agree to receive recurring automated reminder messages from TextMe Reminders. Message frequency varies based on the reminders you create. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.
