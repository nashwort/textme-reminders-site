Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

TextMe Reminders is a personal reminder service operated by TextMe Reminders. This Privacy Policy explains what information the service may collect, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

Information we collect

Depending on how the service is used, TextMe Reminders may collect:

  • Mobile phone number and SMS opt-in status.
  • Reminder titles, reminder text, dates, times, recurrence settings, and completion or snooze status.
  • Message metadata, such as delivery status, timestamps, and user replies.
  • Basic account information necessary to provide the service.
  • Technical information needed for security, reliability, and troubleshooting.

How we use information

We use information only as reasonably necessary to operate and improve TextMe Reminders, including to:

  • Send reminders requested by the user.
  • Process commands such as START, STOP, HELP, DONE, and SNOOZE.
  • Maintain reminder history and prevent duplicate messages.
  • Secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service.
  • Comply with applicable legal and telecommunications requirements.

SMS data and marketing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

We may share information with service providers only when necessary to operate the service, such as telecommunications, cloud hosting, database, and security providers. These providers receive only the information needed to perform services on our behalf.

SMS opt-out

You can opt out of TextMe Reminders SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. You may reply HELP for assistance. After opting out, you will not receive further reminder texts unless you opt in again.

Data retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and satisfy legal or regulatory obligations. Reminder and message records may be deleted or anonymized when they are no longer needed.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children

TextMe Reminders is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization. If you believe information was provided improperly, contact us so it can be reviewed.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the service changes. The “Last updated” date above will be revised when material changes are made.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or TextMe Reminders can be sent to TextMeReminders@gmail.com.
